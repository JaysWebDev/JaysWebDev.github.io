What Changed This Month

Scoring engine v1.5.0

Five ownership/valuation signals now feed the signal score: corporate owner, ownership cluster, recent transfer, assessment gap (AVM), and vintage building. Buildings with strong signals rank meaningfully higher than in spring runs.

Stale-sale cutoff tightened

Cap-rate valuations now require a sale from 2021 or later (was 2018). Older sales fall back to the automated valuation model, which removes most inflated cap rates caused by pre-COVID purchase prices.

Valuation transparency

Every deal now carries a valuation source tag — recent sale, AVM, or assessed value — so you can see exactly what the cap rate denominator is built on.

Methodology. Opportunity scores combine estimated cap rate (HUD Fair Market Rent income model against recent-sale, AVM, or assessed valuations), ownership and valuation signals, and data-quality confidence. Rent estimates are conservative federal FMR baselines, not market comps. Full leaderboard: jays.website/rental/scored_opportunities.html
